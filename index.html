Personal engine · 2025–present

RetroEngine

A DirectX 12 engine I'm writing solo from scratch. Its CRT simulation works from first principles, per pixel: beam scanning, phosphor emission and decay, persistence, and glass diffusion. It isn't a post-process filter.

  • Stable 60 FPS on a Radeon 660M integrated GPU, before instancing or culling
  • ECS, terrain streaming, shared-ownership texture cache
  • ImGui editor with a live GPU memory debug panel

A space dogfighting game made by a two-person team; Josh Chapman and I split the work 50/50. FlashPoint Games published it. On my side: rendering and effects, plus content and Endless Mode.
